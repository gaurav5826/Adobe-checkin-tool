# Check-in Conversation Prep

Turns the written Check-in into a plan for the live conversation. Advisory only.

## Input
The drafted Answers (`output/<cycle>/answers.md`) or the user's highlights. Read
`config/content-preferences.md` for tone and altitude.

## Produce
1. **Lead with (3-4 talking points).** The highest-impact things to open with, in
   plain spoken language — impact first, one line each.
2. **Likely questions & responses.** 4-6 questions a manager might ask — e.g.
   "what was hardest?", "where are you behind and why?", "what's next?", "what do
   you need from me?" — each with a crisp, honest suggested answer drawn from the
   evidence. Include how to speak to any **behind/in-progress** work plainly.
3. **Questions to ask your manager.** 2-3 useful, level-appropriate questions
   (priorities, specific feedback, what growth looks like next). Per
   content-preferences, for early-career avoid asks that read as reaching
   (lead/mentor) or as wanting to leave the project (more cross-team).
4. **Steering growth/feedback (Q2/Q3) verbally.** A line or two: own a growth area
   honestly; keep manager feedback constructive and specific.

## Guardrails
- A rehearsal aid — concise, honest, level-appropriate; don't inflate.
- Match the written answers; introduce no claim that isn't in the evidence.
